About Our Mission

FeelineFrenz specializes in critical medical interventions and dedicated sheltering operations. Our focus remains strictly clinical and humane: addressing severe injuries, administering essential vaccinations, and providing an environment where stray populations receive clinical grade care and a chance at stable life or safe re-release.
